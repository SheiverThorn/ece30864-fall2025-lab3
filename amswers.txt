Question1: What are some Pros and Cons of Agile development (vs Non-Agile)?

Pros: Flexibility of the development. 
      Customer involves much more during development.
Cons: Hard to predict the result.
      Requires more times to communicate and decisions.

Question2: Explain the role and responsibilites of the Scrum Master vs the Product Owner

Scrum Master is a facilitator and coach of the development team. Responsible for team to work together and
making an enviornment for team to collaborate.

Product Owner is a visionary of customers. Responsible for defining product features and ensuring products meets
customer's requirments.

Question3: What are the 3 types of meetings that happens during sprint cycle?

1. Daily-standup meetings.
    a. What teammember did previous day.
    b. What teammember will try to achieve today.
2. Sprint review meetings
    a. Presentation for the product.
    b. Review previous sprint and planning for next sprint.
3. Sptrint retrospective meetings
    a. Analyzing previous sprint cylce.
    b. Discussing which tasks they will stop, and start doing for work efficiency.
